# Sorting & QC

*Poster for: the sorting table. Keep the colour reference board of each colour within reach.*

1. Scan the **production card** - the screen opens on Sorting & QC.
2. Sort every piece: **Grade A**, **Grade B** or **Reject**.
3. Enter the three counts on the keypad.
4. Take a sample of the batch and tap the **level** you see for colour, texture, size, pits, crazing and iron spots.
5. Compare with the **approved colour reference**. Tap **Yes** or **No - hold the lot**.
6. Enter the inspector's name.
7. Press **Save**. Dashed levels = outside the standard: the lot goes on **QC hold**.
8. Keep grade B pieces apart from grade A - they are packed separately.

## The standard (glazed)

Colour ≤ 3 medium · texture ≤ 3 medium · size ≤ 3 heavy · pits / chips ≤ 2 prevalent · crazing ≤ 2 prevalent · iron spots ≤ 1. Dark glazes: pits and crazing one level more. Natural: colour ≤ 2, crazing ≤ 1.
