# Daily routines

The system only knows what people record. Ten minutes a day per role keeps it true.

## Sales - every morning

1. Open the **Dashboard** and clear *Needs attention*: orders waiting for a gate, quotes about to expire, samples to follow up, overdue invoices, claims.
2. Work through [My tasks](/tasks), overdue first.
3. Record every payment received yesterday (**Record payment** on the order) - deposits open the production gate automatically.
4. Record written colour approvals the day they arrive (order → Gates → Record) and attach the email.
5. Log calls and emails on the client timeline in two lines.

Weekly: review the [Pipeline](/pipeline) - move or close stale opportunities, record the reason for lost ones. Check exchange rates monthly (Settings → Sales defaults).

## Production manager - every morning

1. [Production board](/production): late cards (red), holds (amber), planned MOs to release.
2. **Release** the MOs to start today and print their **production cards**.
3. [Kilns](/production/kilns): what is ready to fire, which kiln is free.
4. [Quality & lots](/production/qc): decide on every lot on hold.
5. [Planning → Materials needed](/production/planning): order anything short **before** the batch reaches that stage.

Weekly: [Planning → Export reserve](/production/planning?tab=reserve) - create MOs for fast sellers below target. Review the scrap rate per stage and the labour report.

## Shop floor - every shift

1. Scan the production card at the end of each stage or shift and record good pieces and scrap (poster: *Record work on the shop floor*).
2. The batch moves **with its card**.
3. Kiln operator: every load is started, fired and unloaded in the system (poster at the kiln).
4. Sorting: grades and the QC check for every batch.
5. Packing: carton labels on every carton, two labels per pallet.

## Warehouse - every day

1. [Put away](/warehouse/putaway) everything in the packing-out area before the end of the day.
2. Receive raw material deliveries into their bays and label them.
3. Pick the orders on the pick lists; load trucks by scanning only.
4. Count **two or three locations** (cycle count). Fix any *no location* units.

Monthly: count the raw-material bays and the quarantine zone.

## Owner / admin - every week

1. Dashboard: sales of the last 12 months, open orders and their gates, free stock by colour.
2. Overdue invoices and open claims.
3. **Download a backup** (Settings → Data & backup) and keep it off the server.
4. Check that costs and prices are up to date for new products (Catalogue shows tiles without a cost).
