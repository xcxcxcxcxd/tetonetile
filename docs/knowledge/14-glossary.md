# Glossary

| Word | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Bejmat** | Rectangular zellige brick, 4.5 × 14.5 or 5 × 15 cm, about 1.8 cm thick |
| **BOM (bill of materials)** | How much clay, glaze, fuel and packaging one m², piece or box consumes, per stage |
| **Box group** | A stock unit of fewer boxes on a shelf (`BX-…`), as opposed to a full pallet |
| **Carton label** | The label on every carton with the lot QR - read by installers and clients |
| **Colour approval** | The client's **written** approval of the colour - one of the two gates before production |
| **Contract price** | A fixed price for one client on one product; wins over list prices |
| **Credit note** | The document that corrects or refunds an issued invoice (`CN-…`) |
| **Cycle count** | Counting a few locations regularly instead of one yearly stocktake |
| **DCOF** | Dynamic coefficient of friction - slip resistance of a floor tile |
| **Deposit** | The share of the order paid before production (50 % by default) - the second gate |
| **Export reserve** | Stock of fast-selling colours kept for small export buyers who cannot wait for production |
| **FCA / CFR / DAP / DDP / EXW** | Incoterms - who pays and carries the risk of transport. FCA Tanger Med is our default; avoid DDP |
| **Gate** | A condition that must be met before production starts: written colour approval and deposit |
| **Grade A / B** | First choice / second choice after sorting. Grade B is packed and sold separately |
| **Groupage** | A shared truck for small loads (up to about 4 pallets) |
| **Incoterm place** | Where the goods change hands, e.g. Tanger Med |
| **Kiln load** | One firing of one kiln (`KL-…`), with the batches in it, temperature and fuel |
| **Lead time** | Weeks from the later of colour approval and deposit to the ready date |
| **Location / position** | A place stock can be: `A01-2B` = zone A, rack 01, level 2, position B |
| **Lot** | One colour batch (`L26-0012`) - one MO, produced and shipped together. Printed on every carton |
| **MO (manufacturing order)** | The order to produce one lot of one product (`MO-…`), with its production card |
| **Natural zellige** | Unglazed terracotta zellige; porous, sealed, never soaked before setting |
| **Overage** | Extra quantity over the measured area for cuts, breakage and repairs - 15 % minimum |
| **Packing-out area** | Where new pallets wait after packing (`IN01`) until they are put away |
| **Pallet** | A stock unit of full boxes of one lot (`PAL-…`), 78 boxes for zellige, 60 for cement |
| **Plint** | Skirting piece 5 × 15 cm, 1.2 cm thick |
| **Pool** | The use of free stock: general, reserved export stock or samples |
| **Production card** | The A5 card with the MO QR that travels with the batch |
| **Proforma** | A pro-forma invoice, usually for the deposit (`PF-…`) |
| **Put-away** | Scanning a pallet then the location where it is stored |
| **Quarantine** | Zone Q, where pallets of lots on QC hold are kept - never picked |
| **QC hold** | A lot blocked after an inspection outside the standard or a colour mismatch |
| **Reserved** | Stock allocated to an order line - not offered to anyone else |
| **Scrap** | Pieces lost at a stage, with a reason |
| **Stage** | A step of the production route: clay, shaping, drying, glazing, firing, chiselling, sorting, packing |
| **Tier** | Price list level: end user, trade, wholesale, programme - a multiplier on cost and a minimum order |
| **Variation & Imperfection standard** | The published limits for colour, texture, size, pits, crazing and iron spots (Material Specification §06) |
