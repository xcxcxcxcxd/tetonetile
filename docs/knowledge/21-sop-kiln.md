# Loading and unloading a kiln

*Poster for: each kiln.*

1. Scan the **kiln door label** and press **Start a load**.
2. For each batch on the trolley, press **Add** and enter the pieces - stay within the pieces that **fit**.
3. Check every batch on the trolley is in the load list. No card, no firing.
4. Press **Start firing**.
5. When the fire is out, press **Firing finished**: enter the peak temperature and the olive pits used.
6. Let the kiln cool. Do not open early.
7. Press **Unload & count**: for each batch enter the **good** and **defective** pieces and the defect.
8. Press **Unload and record**, then move each batch with its card to chiselling.

## Why

Colour changes from one kiln load to the next. The load number, temperature and fuel are part of the lot's history - they explain shade differences and help answer claims.

## If something is wrong

- *Kiln over capacity* - do not overload: an overloaded kiln fires unevenly. Take pieces off.
- Good + defective must equal what was loaded.
