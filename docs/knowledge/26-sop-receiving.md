# Receiving raw materials

*Poster for: the raw material store.*

1. Check the delivery note against the **purchase order**: material, quantity, condition.
2. Open the purchase order (Purchasing) and press **Receive delivery** - or scan the **bay label** and press Receive.
3. Enter the quantity received per line and the **supplier lot / delivery note** number.
4. Scan or type the **bay** where it is stored (M01, M02…).
5. Press **Receive** and print the **lot labels**.
6. Stick a lot label on each stack, drum or pallet of the delivery.
7. Store new lots **behind** the old ones - the oldest is used first.

## If something is wrong

- Short or damaged delivery: receive what arrived; the PO stays *part received*. Note the problem on the PO.
- Wrong glaze colour: do not receive it into the glaze bay - set it aside and tell production.
