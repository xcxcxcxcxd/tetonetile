# Loading a container or truck

*Poster for: the loading dock.*

1. Take the **loading sheet** of the shipment to the dock.
2. Scan its QR and press **Scan to load**.
3. Scan **every pallet label** before it goes in. A refused pallet stays on the dock - tell the office.
4. Photograph **each row** of pallets in the container.
5. When the list is empty, photograph the **closed doors and the seal**.
6. Press **Loading finished** and enter the **seal number**.
7. Add the photos on the shipment (camera button).
8. Give the signed delivery note / CMR to the office.

## Why

The loading scan is the last check that the right lot goes to the right client. Photos and the seal number answer transit-breakage claims.
