# Claims

Handmade zellige generates questions after delivery. A claim handled with **facts** - the lot, the QC record, the loading photos - is short and fair for both sides.

## The conditions every client accepted

From the sale conditions printed on every quote:

- Handmade material: variation in colour, shade, size, thickness and surface is **inherent and not a defect** (Material Specification §06).
- Quantities include **overage** for cutting, transit breakage and future repairs.
- Samples are **indicative**; colour varies between kiln loads; a later order will not match.
- **Inspect on arrival. Claims must be made in writing before installation; installation constitutes acceptance.**

## 1. Record the claim

[Claims](/claims) → **New claim** (or from the client page):

- **Client** and **order**; the **lot** from the carton label (the client can read it or send a photo);
- **Type**: transit breakage, quantity shortage, wrong reference, colour / batch, quality (outside the standard), other;
- **Reported on**, **in writing?**, **before installation?** - these two decide whether the claim is within the conditions;
- **Quantity affected** and what the client reports;
- **Photos** - pallets on arrival, damaged boxes, carton labels, the installed surface.

The claim page says immediately whether it is **within the conditions** or not.

## 2. Investigate

Press **Investigate** and check, depending on the type:

| Type | Check |
|---|---|
| Transit breakage | Overage on the order (15 % minimum covers normal breakage). Photos of the pallets on arrival. Our loading photos and seal number on the shipment |
| Shortage | Packing list and the loading scan (every pallet is scanned at loading). Delivery note signed by the receiver |
| Wrong reference | Carton labels against the order - the loading scan refuses pallets of other orders |
| Colour / batch | Was one lot delivered per line (packing list)? Was the colour approved in writing? The lot's QC record |
| Quality | The **lot page**: QC levels recorded at sorting against the Variation & Imperfection standard, kiln loads, inspector |

Open the lot from the claim to see the whole history. If a real problem is found, **put the lot on hold** - every pallet still in stock is blocked at once.

## 3. Decide

- **Accept** - write what was agreed in *Resolution* (and the credit amount).
- **Reject** - write why, referring to the conditions or the standard. Outside the conditions (installed, not in writing) any settlement is goodwill.

## 4. Settle

| Settlement | How |
|---|---|
| **Credit note** | On the claim, **Credit note** next to the order's invoice: a draft credit note is created from the invoice - reduce it to the accepted quantity and issue it |
| **Replacement tiles from stock** | Sell or give the boxes on a new order (price 0 if agreed) - from the **same lot** if any is left |
| **Replacement batch** | **Replacement batch** creates an MO (purpose *rework / replacement*). Warn the client that a new batch will not match exactly |

Then **Mark resolved**.

## Learn from it

Every claim is linked to its lot, shipment and client. Repeated breakage on one route means packing or loading must change; repeated colour claims on one glaze mean the kiln or the glaze needs attention. Review open and resolved claims monthly.
