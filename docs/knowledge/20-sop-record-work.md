# Record work on the shop floor

*Poster for: clay preparation, shaping, drying racks, glazing, chiselling.*

1. Scan the QR on the **production card** clipped to the batch.
2. Check the **stage** shown at the top (it is pre-selected).
3. Tap **Good pieces** and type the number on the keypad - or tap **All waiting**.
4. Tap **Scrap**, type the broken pieces and tap the **reason**.
5. Tap your name under **Who did the work?**
6. Tick **stage finished** only when nothing more of this batch will come.
7. Press **Save** and read the next stage on the screen.
8. Move the batch **with its card** to the next stage.

## If something is wrong

- *"Only N pieces are waiting"* - count again; you cannot record more than arrived.
- *"Pieces still waiting"* when finishing - count again before accepting a shortfall.
- **No card on a batch?** Stop and ask the production manager. Never work a batch without its card.
- **No network?** Write the numbers in the table on the card and record them as soon as you can.
