# Installation & care of handmade zellige

**Read this guide in full, with your installer, before installation begins.** Zellige is set by hand, and the decisions that matter most are made before the first tile touches mortar.

Every piece was shaped, glazed and chiselled by hand. Expect variation in colour, shape and size within your order - your sample showed one batch, not a standard. If you need a uniform surface, choose a different tile.

> **Natural (unglazed) zellige** follows a different sequence at several steps - they are marked **Natural** below.

## 1. Inspect your order on arrival

- Check every box **before installation**: colour reference and format against your order confirmation, quantity, transit damage. **Claims must be made in writing before installation - installation constitutes acceptance.**
- About **15–20 % of pieces** show crazing (hairline cracks), chipped edges or pits exposing the clay. These are not defects; matching grout fills the pits.
- Dust, shipping residue and ink marks from cutting are normal - they come off with a damp cloth.
- Tiles can absorb moisture in transit, which darkens the colour temporarily. Lay them out to dry fully before setting.
- **Overage**: at least 15 %; 20–30 % if your installer will set aside heavily pitted pieces. Keep the leftover boxes for future repairs - a later order will not match.
- Keep the **lot number** printed on the carton labels. Quote it for any question or repair.

## 2. Blend and dry-run

- **Open all boxes and mix tiles from every box** before setting. Installing box by box produces visible banding.
- Lay the tiles out in the room (dry run) and review them, ideally with mock-up boards under the project's final lighting. Direct light makes zellige brighter; shade reads darker.
- Measure pieces before planning a pattern - never assume identical dimensions.
- Choose the grout colour now: a matched grout is recommended; a contrasting grout highlights crazing and pits and needs a pre-grout sealer.

## 3. Tools and materials

- Premium **modified thin-set** to ANSI A118 / ISO 13007 - **no mastic**
- Notched trowel, levelling wedge spacers, rubber grout float, sponge and bucket
- Water-cooled tile saw with a diamond blade
- Penetrating sealer (e.g. 511 Porous Plus) - wet areas, exteriors, contrasting grout and all natural zellige
- Grout: e.g. Mapei Ultracolor Plus FA for glazed, Mapei Flexcolor CQ for natural
- Natural only: matte protective wax (e.g. FILA Matte Wax) - interior only
- pH-neutral cleaner, lint-free cloths, paint roller and tray
- Face covering and eye protection

Equivalent regional products may be used if the installer follows that manufacturer's instructions.

## 4. Setting the tiles

1. **Soak glazed tiles** 1–2 minutes in room-temperature water to remove dust and improve adhesion. **Natural: do not soak** - it draws salts to the surface (efflorescence that is very hard to remove).
2. **Substrate**: level, clean, and waterproofed in wet areas.
3. **Thin-set**: spread with a notched trowel and **back-butter every tile**, in the same direction, to avoid voids.
4. **Level as you go**: thickness varies (≈ 0.95 cm squares, 1.8 cm bejmat, 1.2 cm plint), so use more or less thin-set behind each tile to keep the surface flush. Press each tile firmly by hand.
5. **Joints - walls**: traditionally butt-jointed (edge to edge) or with a minimal 1.5 mm (1/16″) joint.
6. **Joints - floors**: 1.5 mm recommended; wider joints or a staggered layout on wet or commercial floors for drainage and grip. Untreated glazed zellige is for **dry floors only**.
7. **Natural**: work in sections of about 60 × 60 cm and wipe thin-set off the face immediately - it cannot be removed from raw clay once cured.
8. **Cure** at least **48 hours** before sealing or grouting.

## 5. Pre-grout sealing

- **Natural zellige - always**: apply penetrating sealer in thin coats until saturated; wipe off excess immediately (over-application leaves a waxy film). **Let it cure fully, about 48 hours, before grouting** - grout over uncured sealer stains the clay permanently.
- **Glazed zellige** in showers, pools, spas, fountains, exteriors, or with contrasting grout: same penetrating sealer before grouting, fully cured.
- Glazed zellige in dry interior areas needs no sealing.

## 6. Grouting

- Do not dry-stack zellige: grout protects the fragile edges and fills the surface pits.
- Press grout deep into all joints, gaps, pits and chips with a rubber float.
- Wipe excess with a damp sponge **immediately** - dried grout is very hard to remove.

## 7. Top coat (natural zellige, interior)

Clean and dry the surface completely, then apply matte protective wax in thin coats, wiping off excess before it dries. Allow 1–2 days before use. **Never use wax outdoors** - UV discolours it; outdoors, re-seal with penetrating sealer after grouting.

## 8. Trim and edges

No bullnose or quarter-round is produced. Install floor-to-ceiling, or bring the wall surface flush with the tile; **mitre outside corners at 45°**; use the **plint** (5 × 15 cm, 1.2 cm thick, every colour) as a skirting; or a metal profile if trim is required.

## 9. Care and maintenance

- Sweep or vacuum floors regularly - abrasive grit scratches the surface.
- Clean with a **mild pH-neutral** cleaner; test on a small area first.
- **Never** use acidic, bleach-based or abrasive cleaners; no steam cleaners on natural zellige.
- Clean spills straight away, especially oil and grease - stains on natural zellige can be permanent.
- **Re-seal** natural zellige, and glazed zellige in wet areas, every 1–2 years or more often with heavy use.
- A natural patina and traffic pattern develop with use - that is part of the material.

## Safety

Wear a face covering and eye protection when unpacking, cutting and installing tiles, and when working with mortar, sealers and grout. Cut outdoors or in a well-ventilated area. Wash hands before eating.
