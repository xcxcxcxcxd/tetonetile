# Packing and labelling

*Poster for: the packing station.*

1. Scan the **production card** - the screen opens on Packing.
2. Check the grade A and grade B pieces to pack (pre-filled with what was sorted).
3. Pack **full boxes only**, in sawdust. Never mix grade A and grade B, never mix two lots.
4. Stick a **carton label** (lot QR) on every carton.
5. Build pallets of **78 boxes** (zellige) or **60** (cement) on 100 × 120 cm pallets; wrap them.
6. Press **Pack and create pallets**.
7. Print the **pallet labels** - two per pallet, front and side, on the film.
8. Leave the pallets in the packing-out area and **put them away by scanning**.

## Remember

- The lot number on the cartons is how the client, the installer and we trace the batch. No carton leaves without one.
- Pallets made for a client order are reserved for it automatically - unless the lot is on QC hold (they go to quarantine).
