# Product knowledge

Zellige is a **handmade Moroccan terracotta tile**. Clay from the Fès and Tétouan regions is shaped by hand in wooden moulds, sun-dried, glazed with mineral pigments where a colour is required, fired in traditional kilns using **olive pits** as fuel, then **chiselled** piece by piece to its final size. No press, no extruder, no automated line.

The result: every piece differs slightly in size, thickness, shade and surface. The glaze crazes, edges chip, iron in the clay leaves spots, and the surface catches light unevenly. **These are properties of the material, not faults** - a project that needs a uniform, machine-made surface should not specify zellige.

## Colours and finishes

- **36 standard colours**, each produced in every format (the Colour Range sheet lists the references). The codes are part of the SKU: `ZR10-39` = 10 × 10 in 39 Rabat black.
- **Glazed**: finish from eggshell to gloss within the same production. Pigments are natural and **lead-free**.
- **Natural (unglazed)**: porous, unfinished terracotta - sealing required in most uses.
- Dark and heavily pigmented glazes show pits and chips more, and may be rated one level higher in the standard.

## Ordering quantities

- **Minimum overage 15 %** over the measured surface - cutting, transit breakage, future repairs.
- **20–30 %** when the installer will select pieces (setting aside heavier pitting or chips) or with **contrasting grout** (it highlights crazing and pits; a pre-grout sealer is needed).
- **Repairs**: keep the overage on site. A later order will not match the installed batch.
- Quoted per m² or per sq ft by format and colour group; lead time counted from written colour approval and the deposit.

## Samples, batches and repeat orders

- Several pieces of each reference are sent - **one tile does not represent the range**.
- Colour and size vary between kiln loads: a sample is **indicative only**.
- The full quantity of a colour is produced and shipped as **one batch** (one lot). Splitting a colour across runs shows as **banding**.
- An exact match between different formats in the same glaze cannot be guaranteed, even within one order.

## Installation in one line

An installer experienced with handmade tile; **blend all boxes** and dry-lay first; modified thin-set (no mastic); 1.5 mm joints; glazed tiles soaked 1–2 minutes, natural tiles **never soaked**; sealing as the guide says. Full sequence: [Installation & care](kb:installation-care).
